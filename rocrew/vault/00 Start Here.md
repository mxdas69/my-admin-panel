# 00 Start Here

This vault is what the crew knows about making Roblox games that people find, understand, keep playing and pay for. Every agent reads the pages its role lists before it plans or builds. The owner can open the `vault/` folder in Obsidian to read or edit it. Edits change how the crew works on the next task.

## How a game gets made

1. **Pitch.** Pulse reads the charts and proposes formats that are growing. The owner approves one. See [[12 Live Ops and Launch]] for what the numbers mean.
2. **Plan.** Architect writes the loop, the first five minutes and the contracts. Balance proves the pacing with a simulation. See [[02 The Core Loop]], [[01 The First Five Minutes]] and [[04 Progression and Economy]].
3. **Build.** Builder, Frame, Scenic, Librarian and Merchant build in Studio, one at a time in their own areas. See [[13 Building It Right]], [[05 UI and HUD]], [[06 World and Look]], [[07 Characters, VFX and Assets]] and [[09 Shops and Monetization]].
4. **Test and fix.** Playtest, Kid Tester and Reviewer find problems. Owners fix them, then testers recheck. See [[08 Mobile]] and [[13 Building It Right]].
5. **Ship prep.** Splash makes the icon and thumbnails, Liveops sets up analytics and the update plan, and Producer runs the last gate. See [[11 Thumbnails, Icons and the Store Page]], [[10 Retention and Social]] and [[12 Live Ops and Launch]].

## Where most Roblox games fail

- **The first five minutes.** Players leave before the game gets good → [[01 The First Five Minutes]]
- **The icon and thumbnail.** Without clicks there are no players, however good the game is → [[11 Thumbnails, Icons and the Store Page]]
- **Monetization.** Too pushy and players quit; too soft and the game earns nothing → [[09 Shops and Monetization]]
